Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Summary

CyberNexus Suite is a set of free browser tools. There are no accounts, and we do not ask you for your name, email or any other personal details to use the site.

What stays on your device

Your sounds, drawings and beats are created and processed inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers.

  • Saved beat presets are stored in your browser’s local storage on your own device. You can remove them with the Delete button or by clearing your browser data.
  • Your cookie choice is stored the same way so we do not ask you again on every visit.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted by Render. Like most hosts, it may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in server logs. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own privacy policy.

Fonts

We load the Plus Jakarta Sans font from Google Fonts. When your browser requests it, Google receives your IP address and browser details.

Sponsored links and advertising

Some parts of the site contain links marked “Sponsored”. They open partner offer pages (served through Monetag) in a new tab. Those pages are run by third parties, who may use cookies or similar technologies and collect data under their own privacy policies. We do not control or receive that data.

We also show a banner-style advertisement from Monetag. It loads from Monetag’s servers and may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads. For visitors in Europe it loads only after you allow advertising. If you change your mind, use “Cookie settings”; your new choice applies the next time the page loads.

Cookies and your choices

When you first visit from Europe, a banner lets you reject all, accept all or manage choices. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and the site works the same either way. Strictly necessary storage (remembering your choice and your saved beats on your device) does not need consent. For visitors in Europe, advertising and partner cookies stay off unless you allow them. We keep your choice for up to 12 months and then ask again. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of the home page, and you can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Where required, we rely on your consent under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the ePrivacy rules (PECR in the UK). The banner is shown to visitors whose device time zone indicates Europe (the EU, EEA and UK). This check happens in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere for it. Visitors elsewhere are not asked up front, but can use the “Cookie settings” link at any time to turn advertising and partner cookies off.

Analytics

We do not currently run analytics tools. If we add them, we will update this page.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because we do not keep personal data about you on our servers, there is usually nothing to access or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any question. You can withdraw consent at any time, and you may complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. The form is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party service, which delivers it to us by email. We use this information only to reply to you and do not add you to any mailing list. We keep messages only as long as needed to answer you and for our records.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy: please use the contact form.
